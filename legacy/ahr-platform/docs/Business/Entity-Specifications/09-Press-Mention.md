# Entity Specification — Press Mention

**Source lineage:** Entity Catalog, Relationship Model (Phase 4); Content Standards §Press/Press Mentions.

## Description
Any external press or media coverage.

## Responsibility
Documents an external source that refers to internal content — it supplements, never replaces, the Archive's own documented facts.

## Relationships
- **Person, Work, Event, or Organization** — at least one linked entity is mandatory.
- **Organization** — mandatory, as the publishing body.

## Content Standard
- **Required:** at least one linked entity (Person, Work, Event, or Organization), the publishing body (an Organization).
- **Optional:** bilingual excerpt or summary of the coverage.
- **Writing standard:** documents that external coverage exists and what it says — it never replaces the Archive's own documented facts with a journalist's characterization of them.
- **Quality requirement:** Press without a documented, identifiable publisher is not admissible as Press — an anonymous or unverifiable source stays as informal background, not a Press entry.

## Lifecycle
Standard Draft → Review → Verified → Published → Archived sequence.
