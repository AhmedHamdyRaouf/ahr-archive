# Entity Specification — Award

**Source lineage:** Entity Catalog, Relationship Model (Phase 4).

## Description
A prize, honor, or official membership.

## Responsibility
Documents official recognition granted to a person or work by a specific body.

## Relationships
- **Person** — via Contribution or direct link.
- **Organization** — mandatory, the granting body.
