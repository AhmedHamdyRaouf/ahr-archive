## What "100%" Means in This Deliverable
Every item above that depends only on Experience & Design decisions is complete. Three categories of items are correctly left open rather than fabricated, because completing them would mean this audit making a decision outside its authority: **(1)** Brand Identity's literal values (Open Decision B10), **(2)** items already flagged as schema/scope gaps in the frozen Development and Knowledge Base material (Profile Management, Organization contact fields, AI suggestion persistence, notification table), and **(3)** implementation-level literal values (exact motion timing, icon library selection) that belong to Development. All three are fully inventoried in `Backlog.md` and `Design Status Report.md` — nothing is silently missing.
