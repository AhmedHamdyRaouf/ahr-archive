◈ What is Dandana?

Music Rediscovered
From the Heart

"Dandana" is a musical project created by Ahmed Hamdy Raouf to reinterpret well-known songs in a different spirit — simpler in instrumentation, deeper in feeling, and more honest in delivery.

Each Dandana is a new reading of a song we know — but from the perspective of a composer who hears in it what no one else has heard before.

The project continues and the Dandana videos keep coming — because real music never ends.
