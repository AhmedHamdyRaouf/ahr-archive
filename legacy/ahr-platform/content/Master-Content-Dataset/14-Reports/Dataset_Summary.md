## Notable content areas

- **Songs (52):** predominantly collaborations with singer Ali El Haggar (~24 of the 52), plus Medhat Saleh (3), Mohamed Mounir (2 — "Ana Menak Etaalemt" 2015 and "Te3b" 2026), and five self-performed "Dandana" tracks.
- **TV/Film (15):** spans 1999 ("El Shehab") to 2026 ("Had Aqsa" with Mohamed Mounir, Rotana production), including the widely covered 2023 series "Teatro."
- **Theatre (20):** includes the large 2022 Riyadh Season production "Ya Ale3b Ya Aghlas," directed by Ashraf Zaki.
- **Awards (16):** includes an "Oscar-style" Picture Song award for "Fagr Beladi," a UAE Golden Residency, multiple government/institutional honors, 5 jury-member roles, and 3 professional memberships (SACEM, SASERO, Egyptian Musicians Syndicate).
- **Press (49):** heavily weighted toward 2022–2026 coverage of "Te3b" (Mohamed Mounir / Had Aqsa) and "El Saa 12 We Deqiqa" (Ali El Haggar), plus reference entries from Arabic and English Wikipedia, IMDb, ElCinema, and Discogs.

## What was intentionally NOT done

- No English titles were invented for songs that don't have one in the source.
- No specific dates were invented where the source only gives a life-stage description (e.g. "early childhood").
- The ~28 additional songs and ~40 additional theatre works referenced only in aggregate "headline stat" cells (not itemized anywhere) were not fabricated as individual records.
- The live Google Sheet backing the website's dynamic Works page could not be fetched by available tooling and was not treated as identical to the uploaded workbooks — see Validation Report, item C-09.
- Homepage images referenced by the live site were not available in the uploaded archive and are not embedded; they are logged as a missing-media reference only.

See `Validation_Report.md` and `validation_issues.csv` in `13-Validation/` for the complete list of conflicts, gaps, and items needing human review, and `Import_Readiness_Report.md` in `14-Reports/` for what remains before this package can be imported into the AHR Platform CMS.
